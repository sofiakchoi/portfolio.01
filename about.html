ABOUT

(nice to meet you)


Welcome to my site! I am a ( designer ) based on the West Coast. Currently designing at SFMOMA.

Practicing primarily in arts and culture, my work considers how design can exist in conversation with and iterate upon artistic and literary processes. I am drawn to investigate the opportunities introduced by designs status as an intermediary between artists, cultural and educational institutions, and the public. I am always open to discussing new work, especially projects that blur the line between printed and digital materials.
